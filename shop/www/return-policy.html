Return & Replacement Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

We want you to be happy with your order. If something is not right, you can ask for a return or a replacement within 14 days of your order being shipped. This policy applies to orders delivered in Pakistan.

What can be returned

  • Items must be unused, in the same condition you received them, and in their original packaging with any tags still attached.
  • Every item in your order can be returned, whether it was new or preloved.
  • If your item arrived damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, contact us and we will make it right.

How to start a return

  1. Sign in with the email you used at checkout and open Account → Your orders.
  2. Choose the item, select Return or Replacement, and tell us what went wrong.
  3. We will review the request and reply by email with the next step.

If you checked out as a guest or cannot find the order, write to us at relooppk@gmail.com and we will help.

Pickup and return shipping

We can arrange the pickup, but the return shipping cost is paid by the customer. Our standard return shipping charge is PKR 350, paid to the courier. We do not charge a restocking fee. Please keep the item packed safely until the courier collects it.

Delivery charges

Standard delivery is PKR 350. Delivery is free on orders above PKR 10,000.

Refunds and replacements

Once we receive the item and check that it is in a returnable condition, we will refund the price you paid to your original payment method, or send the replacement you asked for. We will contact you if the item is not in a returnable condition before taking any action.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your order arrived damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, contact us within the same 14-day window. We will arrange a replacement or a refund, including the return, at no cost to you.
